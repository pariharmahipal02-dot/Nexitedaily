Welcome to NexiteDaily. We value your trust and are committed to protecting your personal information and providing transparent grocery delivery rules under Indian Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules and Google Play Store policies.

1. Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Information We Collect: When you create an account or place an order on NexiteDaily, we collect only the details strictly needed for order fulfilment: your Name, Mobile Number, Email Address (if signed in), Delivery Address, Pincode, and GPS Coordinates (only when you grant permission to pin your delivery location).

How We Use Your Data:

  • To verify serviceable delivery zones, pack your grocery orders accurately, and navigate our delivery partner to your doorstep.
  • To send order status updates, delivery OTPs, and customer support responses.
  • We never sell or rent your personal data to third-party advertisers.

Payment Security: All online UPI/Card payments are processed through verified payment partners (Razorpay / NPCI UPI apps) using 256-bit SSL encryption. NexiteDaily does not store your debit/credit card numbers, CVV, or UPI PIN.

Account & Data Deletion: You can request complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting our Grievance Officer via email or WhatsApp below. Requests are completed within 7 working days.

2. Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

  • Open Box Check: Customers can inspect item quality, packaging seal, and weight at the time of delivery before completing Cash on Delivery or UPI payment.
  • Order Cancellation: Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are marked "Out for Delivery" by contacting our helpline/WhatsApp support.
  • Damaged, Expired, or Missing Items: If any item is damaged, leaked, expired, or missing, report it within 24 hours of delivery (within 4 hours for perishable items like milk, bread, or vegetables) with a clear photo on WhatsApp support.
  • Refund Timeline: Approved refunds for online/UPI payments are processed back to the original payment method or UPI ID within 3 to 5 business days, or adjusted instantly as a replacement on request.

3. Terms & Conditions

  • Delivery is currently limited to active serviceable pincodes displayed inside the app.
  • Product prices and stock availability update in real time. Prices displayed at checkout are final and inclusive of applicable taxes.
  • For high-value first-time Cash on Delivery orders (₹1,000+), our store team may place a quick confirmation call before dispatch.
  • All food and grocery products are sourced and handled in compliance with applicable FSSAI food safety standards.
