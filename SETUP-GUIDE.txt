STEP 1: FIREBASE CONSOLE SETUP
1. Open Firebase Console -> project 'nexitedaily'.
2. Firestore Database -> Rules -> paste contents of 'firestore.rules' -> Click PUBLISH.
3. Authentication -> Sign-in method -> Enable 'Email/Password' AND 'Google'.
4. Authentication -> Settings -> Authorized domains -> Add your live Vercel/custom domain.

STEP 2: DEPLOY ON VERCEL / NETLIFY
1. Extract this ZIP and upload all files/folders directly to a GitHub repository.
2. Import that repository in Vercel (or Netlify) and Deploy.
3. Open https://your-domain/admin.html and login with your Admin UID email/password.
4. Go to 'Zones & Settings' tab -> Save your Pincodes, Business UPI ID, WhatsApp Number, and (optional) Razorpay Key ID.
5. Go to 'Catalog & Stock' tab -> Click 'Bulk CSV Upload' and upload 'sample-catalog.csv' to test live products immediately!

STEP 3: CONVERT TO PLAY STORE .AAB (PWABUILDER)
1. Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com and enter your live HTTPS URL.
2. Click 'Package for Stores' -> Android -> All Settings.
3. Package ID: in.nexitedaily.app | App Name: NexiteDaily | Theme Color: #14532d.
4. IMPORTANT: Under Features, TURN ON 'Location Delegation' so GPS works natively inside the Android App.
5. Generate & download the package (keep signing.keystore & signing-key-info.txt safe in Google Drive!).

STEP 4: GOOGLE PLAY CONSOLE UPLOAD
1. Upload the generated '.aab' file in Play Console -> Closed Testing (12 testers for 14 days for personal accounts).
2. Store Listing Assets:
   - App Icon: icons/icon-512.png
   - Feature Graphic: assets/playstore-feature-graphic-1024x500.png
   - Privacy Policy URL: https://your-domain/privacy.html
   - Account Deletion URL: https://your-domain/privacy.html#delete-account
3. Copy SHA-256 Fingerprint from Play Console -> App Integrity -> App Signing.
4. Paste SHA-256 into '.well-known/assetlinks.json' and re-deploy on Vercel (removes browser URL bar inside app).
